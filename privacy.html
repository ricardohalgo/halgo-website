halgo — Privacy Notice for Private Testing

Version 1, 3 October 2026

This is the privacy notice for halgo's private test, open to invited people only. It is not the full privacy policy: that will replace it before halgo opens more widely.

Who runs halgo

Ricardo Covas, personally — there is no company yet. Contact: info@halgo.ch. halgo is invite-only: you're reading this because you were personally invited.

What halgo is

A free website, app.halgo.ch, that analyses your own training and health data. It's online only — there is no app to install.

Where your data lives

On a server at Hetzner, in Nuremberg, Germany (EU). Your data is kept in its own separate store: no other tester can see it, and you can't see theirs. Backups are encrypted and also kept at Hetzner, in the same region, rotated for up to 6 months.

Being straightforward about today's limits

The server's disk is not encrypted yet. That means Ricardo, who runs the server, could technically access your data. The goal is for that to stop being possible. Until then, he does not look at anyone's data unless you ask him for help with something — and every time he does, it's recorded.

What comes in — only what you choose to connect or upload

  • Apple Health data: either an export you upload yourself, or data sent daily by the Health Auto Export app.
  • Workout files you upload: FIT, GPX, TCX, or a Garmin or Strava archive.
  • If you connect Polar: your workouts, sleep, Nightly Recharge, heart rate, and Cardio Load. ("Polar Ecosystem" is credited as the source wherever this data is shown, as Polar's licence requires.)
  • COROS will work the same way once it's added, under the same rules.

What halgo does with your data

Shows you your own analysis — nothing more. It is never sold, never shared, and there is no advertising. It is never used to train any AI model. No AI model is used in halgo today. Everything halgo shows you is fitness and training guidance, not medical advice.

Other services involved

Sign-in and notice emails are sent through Scaleway, based in France (EU). When a route map is shown, it loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap — on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings. That only reveals the map area being viewed, not who you are. halgo uses only cookies it needs to work: one keeps you signed in, and two short-lived ones are used only while you sign in. There are no analytics or tracking cookies.

What's kept about sign-ins

Sign-in records: 90 days. Ended sessions: 30 days. Technical logs, which never contain health data: 14 days.

How you sign in

There are no passwords. You sign in with a passkey, or with a one-time link sent to your email. The link works once and expires after 15 minutes. You stay signed in for up to 30 days, and you're signed out sooner after 12 hours of inactivity. Before you download your data, delete your account or change how you sign in, halgo asks you to confirm it's you. halgo emails you when a new passkey is added, when your account is signed in on a new device, and when your email changes. Connections to halgo are encrypted (HTTPS).

Your rights, any time

  • Disconnect Polar (or any other connection) — its data is deleted.
  • Download all of your data from the app.
  • Delete your account yourself — this removes your data immediately, and from the backups within 7 days.
  • Or just ask Ricardo (info@halgo.ch) to do any of the above for you.
